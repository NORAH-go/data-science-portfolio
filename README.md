## 🚀 Project Showcase

### Project: E-Commerce Sales Analysis - Impact of Discounts on Profitability
**Status:** Completed ✅

**Research Question:** 
Does a higher discount percentage lead to higher overall profitability, or does it cause financial losses?

**Key Findings:**
- By analyzing the `order_items.csv` dataset, I found a **negative correlation** between discount rates and profit margins.
- Orders with a 0-10% discount yielded the highest average profit (approx. $250). Once the discount exceeded 40%, the average profit dropped dramatically to nearly $50.
- Out of nearly 400,000 order items, about 1.36% resulted in a financial loss, highlighting the risk of aggressive discounting strategies.
